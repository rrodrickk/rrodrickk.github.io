About

I'm 19, born in Braga, Portugal currently in second-year Software Engineering undergraduate at the University of Minho.

Outside campus I study jazz guitar. My favourite genres beyond jazz are neo-soul, blues, barber beats, shoegaze and rock. Inspirations include Miguel Araújo, Ganso, Pat Metheny, Pink Floyd, Khruangbin, Sade, Masayoshi Takanaka, Casiopea… Spotify ↗

I'm the lead guitarist and co-founder of Solmåne, a Rock-Pop band where I arrange, record and perform original material.

My father is a software engineer and I grew up around computers. I aim to pursue a masters in AI / ML and keep building things that matter.
