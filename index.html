I'm 19, born in Braga, Portugal, currently a second-year Software Engineering undergraduate at the University of Minho.

Outside campus I study jazz guitar. My favourite genres beyond jazz are neo-soul, blues, barber beats, shoegaze and rock. Inspirations include Miguel Araújo, Ganso, Pat Metheny, Pink Floyd, Khruangbin, Sade, Masayoshi Takanaka, Casiopea…

I'm also the co-founder of a alternative/indie/rock band Solmåne where I arrange, record and perform original material.

I belive my passion for this field stems from a childhood fascination with how things are built. I wanted to understand what lies behind the abstraction of highly advanced, seamless products. I owe this drive to my father, a software engineer who inspired me to always pursue greatness.

As engineers, we hold real responsibility over the systems we design, from architecture to tooling. That's why I am committed to honing my craft: writing cleaner, faster, and more maintainable solutions that respect both the user and the machine. I always aim to push boundaries. Moving forward, keep building things that matter.

My goal is to pursue a master's in AI/ML and build an international career.
